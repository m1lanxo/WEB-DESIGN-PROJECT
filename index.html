🐉 Our Story

Lucky Dragon was founded in 1998 by the Chen family, who brought their grandmother's recipes all the way from Sichuan province to the heart of New York City.

For over 25 years, we have been welcoming guests like family — offering warm service, generous portions, and dishes that stay with you long after the last bite.

Whether you're a lifelong fan of Chinese cuisine or trying it for the very first time, we make it simple, delicious, and memorable.
